Field manual

How Polaris reads Robinhood Chain, what every number means and where each one comes from

The Drift

The Polaris scanner reads every ERC-20 Transfer from the zero address on Robinhood Chain, which is how a token's first supply appears. Each new contract is dated from its creation transaction, so a token that mints again weeks later never shows up as newborn. Tokens stay on the radar for 48 hours after birth.

Holder counts and transfer counts refresh every few minutes from the chain explorer. The scanner keeps its place by block number, so nothing is skipped across restarts.

North Score

A number from 1 to 99 for how hard a newborn token is pulling the Drift. Every input is on-chain and the formula is open:

holders  = log10(holders + 1) / log10(5000)        capped at 1
mints    = log10(mints seen + 1) / 3                capped at 1
fresh    = 0.2 + 0.8 × (1 − age / 48h)
trust    = 1 if the source is verified, else 0.7

score    = (0.45·holders + 0.30·mints + 0.25·fresh) × trust × 100

On the radar, a token's distance from the centre is 100 minus its score, its bearing comes from its address and its size from its holders. The highest score is the North Pick.

North Vaults

Each North Vault is an ERC-4626 vault behind its own PolarisProxy. Yield is streamed in linearly: when rewards arrive they unlock second by second over the stream, so the share price climbs smoothly and there is no single moment to front-run.

The ranking uses the same three factors for every vault:

depth = clamp(log10(TVL) / 9, 0.3, 1)
exit  = clamp(log10(withdrawable) / 7, 0.3, 1)
score = APY × depth × exit

Before any deposit the terminal checks two things on-chain: the vault is active in PolarisRegistry, and the vault's asset() is the registry asset. The registry refuses to list any vault whose asset does not match. Deposits can be paused by the owner; withdrawals cannot.

On testnet the vaults hold pUSD, a testnet stable anyone can claim once a day from PolarisDollar.drip().

Beacon

One ping per UTC day through PolarisBeacon.ping(). Pinging on consecutive days builds a streak, and a missed day restarts it at one.

xp per ping = 10 + 5 × min(streak − 1, 20)
holder boost = ×1.5 while the wallet holds 10,000 $POLARIS

Every ping emits Pinged(observer, day, streak, xp, gained). The leaderboard is rebuilt from these events, so anyone can recompute it.

Orbit signals · v2.0

Orbit adds eight instruments on top of the North Score. Each one is computed by replaying the token's own Transfer logs from its birth block, so anyone with an RPC can rebuild every number below.

Score history

The North Score recomputed every 15 minutes of a token's life from the holders and mints it had at that moment. Block times come from the token's own birth block and the chain head, because Robinhood Chain logs do not carry reliable timestamps.

Cluster detection

launch bundle = wallets that bought from a pool in the first 30s of trading
split         = one transaction that seeds 3 or more fresh wallets
cluster share = supply still held by bundle and split wallets
level         = high   if cluster share ≥ 30% or top 10 ≥ 70%
                watch  if cluster share ≥ 10% or top 10 ≥ 40%
                clear  otherwise

Deployer profile

Every token is tied to the wallet that sent its creation transaction. The profile lists that wallet's launches in the window and the graded ones from the last 30 days: how much of each supply it sold into pools, the cluster level, and the outcome a day later. Selling is a transfer from the deployer into a known pool after the transfer that seeded it.

Watchlist and alerts

The watchlist lives in your browser. Polaris checks it on a timer and raises an alert for three events:

liquidity opened  the first transfer into any v2, v3 or v4 pool of the token
deployer sold     the deployer sends 0.5%+ of supply into a pool
score drop        the North Score falls 15 points under its last-hour high

Stock-pair integrity

Robinhood publishes the canonical stock token contracts in its asset registry. Every new pool and every newborn token using a stock ticker is checked against that list: the canonical contract is marked as such, anything else using the ticker is an imitation. On testnet the five stock tokens Robinhood issues there are the reference.

Launchpad coverage

A token's launchpad is the contract its creation transaction called; a token deployed straight from a wallet is a direct deploy. Venues are the factories that open pools: Uniswap v2 PairCreated, v3 PoolCreated and v4 PoolManager.Initialize, and every fork that emits the same events. Contracts with verified source carry their name.

Track record

Every token is graded a day after birth against the score it showed at one hour old:

thrived  holders doubled from 1h and reached 25+
rugged   the deployer sold 25%+ into pools, or holders fell below half their peak
faded    everything else

The record groups the grades by early score band, so the open question stays visible: do strong early pulls really last longer.

Holder tier

              watch slots   alert checks   score history
public        5             every 60s      last 6h
holder        25            every 15s      full 48h

holder = the connected wallet holds 10,000 $POLARIS (balance read only, no staking)

Constellation · v3.0

Constellation opens Polaris up: the same signals for your bots and sites, filters you write yourself, a scan of any wallet, and a say in what the score weighs next.

Public API

Every endpoint the terminal reads is public under /api/v1, with CORS open to any origin. Answers are JSON; addresses are lowercase 0x strings.

GET /api/v1/drift?limit=40&launchpad=&rule=   newborn tokens, strongest pull first
GET /api/v1/token?a=<token>                       one token: score history, events, cluster, deployer, pools
GET /api/v1/deployer?a=<wallet>                   a creator's launches and how they went
GET /api/v1/portfolio?a=<wallet>                  risk profile of a wallet's holdings
GET /api/v1/alerts?a=<token,token>               watchlist events
GET /api/v1/record · launchpads · integrity · pulse · governance · health
GET /api/v1/badge?a=<token>                       SVG score badge
              requests a minute   counted per
no key        120                 IP address
key           120                 key
holder key    1,200               key (wallet holds 10,000 $POLARIS)

Get a key in the terminal under council: your wallet signs one message, nothing is sent on-chain. Pass it as the x-polaris-key header or the key query parameter. A key carries its wallet's holder tier everywhere, including the 48h score history. Issuing a new key revokes the old one. Every answer carries x-ratelimit-limit, -remaining and -reset; going over returns 429.

Custom rules

A rule is a list of conditions a token must all pass. Save up to eight in the terminal, filter the radar with one, and get an alert when a new token starts matching. The same text works as the rule parameter of /api/v1/drift.

numbers   score  holders  mints  age (minutes, or 30m 6h 1d)
compare   >=  <=  >  <  =  !=
flags     verified  stock (canonical stock token)  fake (imitates a ticker)  direct (no launchpad)
negate    !fake  !direct

score>=60 holders>=25 age<6h !fake

Portfolio scan

Polaris reads a wallet's balance in every token it knows, in one Multicall3 call: the 48h Drift, every launch graded in the last 30 days and every canonical stock token. Each holding gets a risk level from its own history:

high   imitates a stock ticker, rugged in its first day, cluster level high, or the deployer sold 25%+
watch  cluster level watch, the deployer sold 5%+, North Score under 20, or faded after a day
clear  none of the above; canonical stock tokens are always clear

Score widget

Any site, forum or community page can show a token's live North Score. The badge is an SVG that refreshes every minute and links back to the token in the terminal. Press Embed on any token to copy it:

<a href="https://polaristerminal.tech/app?t=0x…"><img src="https://polaristerminal.tech/api/v1/badge?a=0x…" alt="North Score"></a>

Signal governance

$POLARIS holders decide what the score should weigh next. A ballot spreads 100 points over six signals: holder spread, mint activity, freshness, verified source, cluster detection and deployer history. The wallet signs its ballot (free, no transaction) and it counts with the $POLARIS that wallet holds at the moment of every tally, so selling takes the weight with it. The latest ballot of a wallet replaces its earlier one. Governance steers signals and data sources only: never funds, fees or rewards.

community weight of a signal = Σ (points × $POLARIS held) / Σ $POLARIS held
today's North Score          = holders 45 · mint activity 30 · freshness 25, unverified source ×0.7

Contracts

Every contract is a UUPS implementation behind a PolarisProxy (ERC-1967). Addresses never change across upgrades, and only the owner can authorise one. All sources are verified on the explorer.
